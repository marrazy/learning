Lasagna recipes

Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Ingredients for creamy chicken lasagna arranged on a countertop including chicken breasts tomato sauce lasagna sheets and cheese Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
Pasta sheets arranged on a plate Meanwhile, place chicken in a saucepan with enough water to cover; bring to a boil. Cook until chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear, about 20 minutes. Remove chicken from the saucepan and shred.
Shredded chicken on a cutting board with a fork and knife placed next to it Combine shredded chicken, 1 cup mozzarella cheese, and cream cheese in a large bowl. Dissolve hot water and bouillon in a liquid measure; pour over chicken mixture and stir until well combined.
Shredded chicken and other ingredients mixed in a clear bowl with a spoon Spread 1/3 of the spaghetti sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Cover with 1/2 of the chicken mixture and top with 3 lasagna noodles; repeat. Top with remaining sauce and sprinkle with remaining 1 cup mozzarella cheese.
A casserole dish with unbaked lasagna topped with cheese and tomato sauce Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes.
Creamy chicken lasagna served in a baking dish
Serve and enjoy!
